# Proposal alignment after the saved-CREATE correction

The source is **Zcash Durable Payment Outbox — Project proposal and pre submission MVP plan | 19 October 2026**. The unchanged document and provenance remain under [reference](../reference/). This review incorporates the requested correction to CREATE recovery; it does not modify the historical evidence.

**The missing operation-to-payment binding was confirmed.** Matching recipient/amount/memo, full intent and artifact effects did not establish which payment's CREATE operation produced a response. Two IDs with identical details could cross-adopt different operations. No on-chain loss was demonstrated. The correction requires a durable operation/payment/owner/intent-digest binding before Zallet CREATE is called and exact matching during recovery. Legacy unbound evidence stays quarantined.

The earlier nine-scenario, reorg and saved-CREATE results are **historical qualification of their pre-correction binary**. They do not qualify the corrected executable. Corrected local validation passed: **43 Rust tests (42 library + 1 CLI)**, formatting, strict Clippy with warnings denied, build, and **10 compiled HTTP/CLI smoke checks with zero remote calls**. [Correction results and hashes](../evidence/correction/summary.json). The corrected executable subsequently passed all nine scenarios plus genuine identical-intent A/B CREATE recovery on a fresh disposable stack with the actual verifier. [Corrected real-stack report](corrected-real-stack-acceptance.md). [Historical summary/hashes](../integration/evidence/real-stack-final/summary.json), [current acceptance scope](acceptance-status.md), [recovery contract](recovery.md).

**Exactly one positive Ironwood sender input is supported**, with one recipient, two through eight actions and independently checked change. Multiple positive inputs are unsupported even when aggregate wallet funds are sufficient. Rejection after CREATE or another operation begins—including an ordinary-looking verification/RPC error—may quarantine the whole dedicated wallet. Failure does not authorize clearing ownership, replacing the business ID or attaching a fresh database to that wallet.

## Specification and evidence scope

The codebase contains the real PCZT adapter/verifier, durable intent/artifact/byte storage, API/CLI, chain observations and business ledger. Historical genuine Zallet/Zebra experiments and the executed fresh-root funding recipe remain valid records of what those exact builds did. The runtime pins were Zallet beta.3 source `987382f67e622915228686e9f956c6a9c9a7514c` with Zaino backend, Zebra 6.2.3 source `7121c82ba6795a151523d5b308a19743f4a1ade7`, and librustzcash `1f6bb2072e7fcb142b0d90ff7b267a8699a84818`. The profile is regtest NU6.3/V6/Ironwood, not NU7/public-network support.

The proposal is a conditional product/grant plan. PR 898's open/draft statement is its historical observation, not a current-status claim. The approximately $30,000, 8–10 weeks, external reviewer and support commitments remain proposed work. No independent external review, adoption or grant approval is claimed. Artifact verification by separately compiled software is distinct from external human review.

## Required correction acceptance

Use two IDs A/B with identical recipient/amount/memo and distinct durable operation IDs. Reject A's response for B with no wallet call or state change. B's own bound response may recover only under B's original owner with zero CREATE replay. This passes through both the Engine fixture and production Zallet recovery with Engine/Store; the latter uses synthetic bytes, a static verifier and three local read-only tip/inspect calls for B, so it tests provenance rather than actual Zcash verification. Reject wrong operation/payment/owner/digest and legacy unbound evidence; retain the atomic owner/slot/stage comparisons. No dedicated check-to-commit race-hook test is claimed. Persist the binding before dispatch, not while recovering. The corrected build must also pass unique-txid/duplicate-migration and no-ownership-clearing regressions. The corrected local regression suite passes; the report above records build/test scope and hashes. Separately, genuine A/B recovery passed on the fresh corrected stack with the actual verifier: matching approved intent, distinct pre-dispatch durable bindings, rejection before RPC with state unchanged, and B recovery under its original owner with zero CREATE replay. Both transactions passed independent final verification and mined receiver checks. [Genuine evidence](../integration/evidence/real-stack-corrected-001/bound-create-recovery/summary.json).

## Operational release milestones

These are required acceptance criteria, not implemented protections:

1. **Verified freshness/restore across business ledger + Outbox + wallet.** Use an independently retained freshness/lineage anchor and coordinated backups. Test active WAL, old-but-valid snapshots, business-ledger rollback, wallet/Outbox skew and remote completion after snapshots. Missing/stale/inconsistent/unverified restores must create zero wallet construction and zero submission attempts; live mode must not bypass the eventual gate.
2. **Enforced/verified broadcasting and exclusivity.** Reject enabled/unknown/changed wallet broadcasting and a competing constructor, including another RPC client or Outbox database. Demonstrate actual enforcement rather than relying on the setup operator's configuration.
3. **NU7.** Publish exact supported node/wallet/verifier/upgrade pins and demonstrate actual transactions and relevant failure behavior at that upgrade. NU6.3 evidence cannot close this gate.
4. **Actual expiry/rejection.** Exercise genuine chain expiry and Zebra rejection/uncertainty while preserving identity, bytes, ownership and business accounting; show no replacement pipeline or automatic balance credit.
5. **Broader recovery and reviewed safe errors.** Define evidence/quiescence rules and test RPC/storage/COMMIT failure windows for each permitted prove/sign/extract recovery. Preserve quarantines for unknown operations; neither an ordinary error nor elapsed time may clear ownership. Broader positive-input support requires separate qualification.
6. **External review and broader qualification.** The corrected local and fresh real-stack binary/test scopes and hashes are now published separately. Obtain external review of operation binding, migration, restore freshness and wallet authority, and qualify additional recovery/input profiles separately. Additional safeguards strengthen the boundary; none substitutes for the primary CREATE-origin correction.

The historical controlled reorg restored its original chain and observed paid true → false → true with no construction/submission. An unknown lookup alone was not treated as proof of reversal. That useful experiment does not close the correction or the operational milestones.
